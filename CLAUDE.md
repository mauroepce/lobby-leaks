## Purpose

Open-source platform to triangulate money flows between politicians and companies — investigative / transparency tooling.

## Layout

- Subprojects live under `modules/`.
- Each subproject has its own `CLAUDE.md` (conventions) and `STATUS.md` (live state).
- Root `INDEX.md` tracks all subprojects.
- See `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` for the full pattern explanation.

## Conventions

- Stable conventions go in `CLAUDE.md` files (root and per-subproject).
- Live state goes in `STATUS.md` per subproject.
- Every key decision should land in `STATUS.md` with the **why**, not just the choice.
